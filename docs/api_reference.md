## coordinates

Source: `src/beyond_spacetime/coordinates.py`

```text
Schwarzschild, Eddington-Finkelstein, Kruskal-Szekeres and Penrose coordinates.

Regions of the maximally extended solution (Kruskal T, X):
    I   : X >  |T|   right exterior          II : T >  |X|  future interior (black hole)
    III : X < -|T|   left exterior           IV : T < -|X|  past interior (white hole)
Singularities: T^2 - X^2 = 1 (II: T>0, IV: T<0). Kruskal null coordinates
U = T - X, V = T + X.  Penrose coordinates: u~ = arctan U, v~ = arctan V,
T_p = v~ + u~, X_p = v~ - u~.
```

## geodesics

Source: `src/beyond_spacetime/geodesics.py`

```text
Radial timelike geodesics of Schwarzschild.

First integral (E = -u_t = f dt/dtau):  (dr/dtau)^2 = E^2 - f(r).
Differentiating gives d^2 r/dtau^2 = -M/r^2 (valid for every E and through the
horizon).  Ingoing Eddington-Finkelstein v = t + r* obeys dv/dtau = 1/(E - rdot),
regular at r = 2M.  Analytic references:
  * E = 1:            tau(r1 -> r2) = 2 (r1^{3/2} - r2^{3/2}) / (3 sqrt(2M))
  * rest at r0 (E^2 = f(r0)): cycloid r = (r0/2)(1 + cos eta),
        tau = (r0/2) sqrt(r0/2M) (eta + sin eta)
  * horizon -> singularity proper time = integral_0^{2M} dr / sqrt(E^2 - f),
        maximal value pi M at E = 0.
```

Tests: `test_schwarzschild.py`  
Experiments: `exp02_infall_tides.py`, `exp07_time_reversal.py`

## causal

Source: `src/beyond_spacetime/causal.py`

```text
Causal structure of the maximally extended Schwarzschild solution and of
a traversable (Ellis-Bronnikov) wormhole.

In Kruskal null coordinates u = T - X, v = T + X the radial (and, by conformal
flatness of the (T, X) sector, the projection of every) future-directed causal

signalling.  Regions I -> III would require v to decrease from v>0 to v<0.
```

## regular

Source: `src/beyond_spacetime/regular.py`

```text
Hayward regular black hole as a documented *effective* (non-fundamental) model.

    f(r) = 1 - 2 m(r)/r,   m(r) = M r^3 / (r^3 + 2 M l^2),   ds^2 = -f dt^2 + dr^2/f + r^2 dOmega^2.

This is a phenomenological metric (Hayward 2006).  It is NOT derived from a
fundamental quantum-gravity theory here; its effective stress-energy is read off
from the Einstein tensor, G^mu_nu = 8 pi T^mu_nu, and is diagnosed against the
classical energy conditions.  A finite curvature invariant is not claimed to be a
complete resolution of the singularity problem.
```

## chronology

Source: `src/beyond_spacetime/chronology.py`

```text

- 2 sqrt2 sinh^2 r dt dphi + dz^2 ]; circles r = const have g_phiphi < 0 for sinh r > 1, i.e.
```
